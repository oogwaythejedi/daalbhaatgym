Pricing FAQ

Money questions, answered

Can I pay with local methods?

Yes — cards, eSewa, Khalti, and bank transfer are all supported. Payments are processed in NPR.

Is there a student discount?

Students with a valid ID get 30% off Pro — email us from your .edu.np address.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — cancel in two taps and keep access until the end of your billing period.

Do you offer family or gym plans?

Gym owners can purchase club packages with 20+ member licenses — contact us for pricing.

Is the Trek Prep plan refundable?

Full refund within 7 days if you haven't started the plan. After that, the plan stays yours forever.
