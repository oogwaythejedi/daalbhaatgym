Food log FAQ

About the database

How are the macros calculated?

Each dish is built from its ingredients, local portion sizes, and Nepali cooking methods — with member corrections and spot checks by our nutrition team.

What if a dish isn't in the database?

Request it and our team adds it within days — community requests drive the database roadmap.

Are the calories for my aamaiko recipe accurate?

Close — home recipes vary. Use the 'adjust serving' tool to fine-tune portions and ingredients.

Can I log drinks?

Chiya, lassi, and even the occasional Khukuri Rum are in the database, logged with honesty and zero judgement.
