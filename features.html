Dashboard FAQ

Using the Today view

How do I log a meal quickly?

Pin your frequent meals at the top — dal bhat, momo, chiura breakfast — and log with two taps.

What happens if I miss a day?

Your streak resets, but earned badges stay. Streak protection (one free day per week) is a Pro feature.

Can I log in Nepali units?

Yes — serving sizes like '1 kachaura', '2 roti', or '1 plate momo (10 pcs)' are all in the database.

How does device sync work?

Connect your watch or fitness account once; steps and sleep sync automatically each time you open the app.
