About

Why dal bhat?

Dal bhat is eaten twice a day by most Nepali households, and 'dal bhat power, 24 hour' is a national saying for a reason — complex carbs, lentil protein, and seasonal vegetables.

The Dal Bhat Story

Fuel that never went to the gym

Global fitness apps treat rice as the enemy and dal as a mystery. But Nepal runs on dal bhat — it powered porters to the summit of Everest and every working day in between. We built a tracker that treats it like the performance fuel it is.

  • Complex carbohydrates for sustained energy
  • Lentil protein for muscle repair
  • Seasonal tarkari for vitamins and fiber
  • Ghee and achar for morale — everything in moderation

What we stand for

The Daal Bhaat Gym code

  • Food is fuel, not shame

    No red bars, no guilt trips. Log your momo, earn your badge, move on.

  • Local first

    Nepali dishes, Nepali servings, Nepali pricing, Nepali and English language.

  • Community over comparison

    Leaderboards with banter, not body-shaming. Clubs are cheering squads first.

  • Built for real conditions

    Low bandwidth, shared phones, treks without signal — the app works where Nepal works.
