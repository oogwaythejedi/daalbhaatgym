Privacy Policy

The fine print for Daal Bhaat Gym.

This privacy policy explains how Daal Bhaat Gym (daalbhaatgym.com) collects, uses, and protects your personal information. This is placeholder legal copy — have it reviewed by a qualified legal professional before launch.

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly (such as your name, email address, and messages submitted through our forms) and basic technical data such as your browser type and pages visited.

2. How we use information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, fulfil orders or bookings, improve our service, and communicate with you where you have asked us to.

In this demo build, form submissions are stored in a local record log on the server running the site. We do not sell your personal information.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us.
