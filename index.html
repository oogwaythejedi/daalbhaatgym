How it works

    Log your dal bhat

  2. 2

    Watch the thali ring fill

    Your daily calories appear as a thali plate: bhaat, dal, and tarkari segments fill as you log.

The thali ring

Your calories, served on a thali

Instead of a boring pie chart, your day is a dal bhat plate — bhaat fills as you eat carbs, dal as you hit protein, tarkari as your fiber climbs. It's the only macro ring that makes you hungry.

  • Bhaat = carbohydrate fuel
  • Dal = plant protein from lentils
  • Tarkari = fiber, vitamins, and greens
  • Achar = the fun stuff (tracked honestly)

Questions

Before you download

Is the app really free?

Yes — logging food and workouts, streaks, and basic challenges are free forever. Pro adds the full food database, offline mode, and advanced plans.

Do you have a Nepali food database?

300+ dishes logged with locally-researched macros — from dal bhat and momo to sel roti and chiura, in Nepali and English.

Does it work offline?

Free users need a connection to sync. Pro users can log offline — built for treks and areas with patchy data.

Can it track steps without a watch?

Yes — the phone's built-in sensors work, and we sync with Apple Watch, Fitbit, Garmin, Google Fit, and Samsung Health.

Is my health data private?

Your logs are private by default. You choose what to share with clubs, challenges, and leaderboards.
