Challenge FAQ

Rules of the arena

Are challenges free?

Member-created clubs and most monthly challenges are free. Premium challenges with prize pools and coach support are Pro features.

How do points work?

Workouts, steps, and balanced meal days earn points; challenge-specific bonuses stack on top. Weekly standings reset every Sunday.

Can I create my own challenge?

Pro users can create private challenges for clubs and squads with custom rules, dates, and prizes.

What are the prizes?

Mostly glory and badges — plus partner prizes from local gyms and sports shops in select premium challenges.
